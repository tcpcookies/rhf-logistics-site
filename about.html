About RHF Logistics

Your reliable freight partner, rooted in Shenzhen and connected to the world.

Who We Are

Shenzhen Ruihuafeng Logistics Supply Chain Co., Ltd. (RHF Logistics) is an international logistics company headquartered in Shenzhen — the heart of China's export manufacturing belt in the Greater Bay Area.

Founded by a team with over a decade of hands-on freight forwarding experience, we help Chinese exporters and overseas buyers move cargo reliably and affordably by ocean, air, express and cross-border trucking — backed by warehousing and customs services.

We believe great logistics is simple: honest quotes, careful handling, proactive communication, and cargo that arrives on time. That is how we build long-term partnerships with our customers.
