ServiceFCL 20GP / 40GP / 40HQ, LCL consolidation

Transit TimeUSA West Coast 15–20 days · Europe 28–35 days · Southeast Asia 5–10 days
DeparturesWeekly fixed sailings, space guarantee in peak season

AirportsSZX Shenzhen · CAN Guangzhou · HKG Hong Kong

OptionsAirport-to-airport, door-to-door DDP, express air
