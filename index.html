FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you pick up goods from any city in China?
Yes. We arrange pickup from suppliers in any Chinese city — Shenzhen, Guangzhou, Dongguan, Yiwu, Shanghai, Ningbo and beyond — and consolidate cargo at our Shenzhen warehouse before export.
Should I choose FCL or LCL sea freight?
As a rule of thumb, if your cargo is more than about 15 CBM, a full container (FCL) is usually cheaper and safer. Below that, LCL consolidation saves cost. We'll calculate both options for you and recommend the best one.
Do you offer DDP / DDU door-to-door service?
Yes. We provide DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) and DDU door-to-door services to major markets including the USA, UK, EU, Canada, Australia and Southeast Asia — you receive the goods at your door without dealing with carriers or customs yourself.
How can I track my shipment?
Every shipment comes with a tracking number (carrier Bill of Lading, air waybill or express number). Our team also sends proactive milestone updates by email or WhatsApp so you always know where your cargo is.
Can you handle batteries, liquids or other sensitive goods?
Yes, we handle a wide range of cargo including built-in and standalone batteries (UN38.3, MSDS), liquids, powders and cosmetics via dedicated channels. Tell us what you're shipping and we'll confirm the right solution.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept T/T bank transfer, PayPal (small amounts), Alipay and WeChat Pay. Payment terms are flexible — typically a deposit before departure and balance against a copy of the bill of lading.
Is cargo insurance available?
Yes. We offer all-risk cargo insurance at competitive premiums (typically around 0.2%–0.3% of the declared value). While incidents are rare, insurance gives you complete peace of mind.
Is there a minimum shipment size?
No minimum. We ship everything from a single document or 0.5 kg parcel by express, up to full 40ft containers and chartered air freight pallets.
